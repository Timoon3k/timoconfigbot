# Optional support replies

- **Installation:** Please describe your installation step, operating system, and the exact error. Share a screenshot with personal information hidden. Never share passwords, license secrets, or tokens.
- **Account linking:** Sign in to TimonConfigs and open Discord account settings. Connect the Discord account you use in this server. Account replacement requires confirmation; support cannot connect an account based on a username alone.
- **Missing VIP access:** Please confirm that your Discord account is linked and check /membership and /sync-status. Staff can review the membership and synchronization audit. A screenshot of a payment redirect does not establish entitlement.
- **Subscription expiration:** Cancellation stops future renewals; already paid access lasts through its expiration date. A failed renewal does not extend the paid period. Lifetime access has no fixed expiration date and remains subject to its license terms.
- **Payment support:** Payments are currently sandbox simulations. Do not send card details or make a real payment through an unapproved checkout. For a future verified transaction, provide only its non-sensitive reference through your existing private support ticket.
- **Troubleshooting:** Please provide the approximate UTC time, the command used, and any public error code. Do not paste .env files, bot tokens, OAuth codes, or private conversations.
